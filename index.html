Reading the tags: the small A / B marker shows which assessment a drug is on. A drug approved for more than one indication is listed once under each matching category, marked i/n — the i‑th of its n total indications on the card. No i/n tag means the card lists only that one indication. The line under each name is the indication exactly as the card phrases it.
What this is: quick, plain-English explanations for the drug-class names and the trickier clinical/indication terms that show up across the deck — the stuff you technically know but might blank on mid-quiz. Not a replacement for your course material, just a fast lookup.
